GENDERED NOUN FORMATTING

When a noun can naturally refer to either a male or female person AND Spanish uses different masculine/feminine forms, represent BOTH forms in `spanish`.

Masculine -o / feminine -a:

* Use `masculine/a`
* Examples: `niño/a`, `maestro/a`, `amigo/a`, `empleado/a`, `cajero/a`
* Do NOT use `(a)` for words ending in `-o`.

Masculine consonant + feminine -a:

* Use `masculine(a)`
* Examples: `trabajador(a)`, `profesor(a)`, `vendedor(a)`, `autor(a)`, `director(a)`

Irregular/substantially different forms:

* Use actual forms separated by `/` with NO spaces.
* Examples: `actor/actriz`, `rey/reina`, `héroe/heroína`
* Never invent forms such as `actor(a)`, `rey(a)`, `héroe(a)`, `actor/actora`, `rey/reya`.

Gender-specific nouns remain separate:

* `hombre`, `padre`, `esposo` → masculine
* `mujer`, `madre`, `esposa` → feminine

Do NOT combine distinct lexical entries such as `rey = King` and `reina = Queen` into one entry.

COMMON VS. EPICENE NOUNS

`common`: form changes according to the sex of the person.

* `niño/a`
* `trabajador(a)`
* `profesor(a)`

`epicene`: one fixed grammatical gender can refer to either sex.

* `persona` → F
* `víctima` → F
* `testigo` → M
* `miembro` → M

Do NOT use `U` for epicene nouns.

gender

* Populate ONLY when `part_of_speech = 1`.
* `M`, `F`, or `U`.
* Common nouns represented with both forms → `U`.
* Epicene nouns → grammatical gender (`M` or `F`).
* Otherwise `null`.

gender_type

* Populate ONLY when `part_of_speech = 1`.
* `regular`: fixed grammatical gender, not epicene.
* `common`: masculine/feminine forms can refer to either sex.
* `epicene`: fixed grammatical gender can refer to either sex.
* Otherwise `null`.

ADJECTIVE GENDER FORMATTING

Only modify `spanish`.

* Masculine `-o` / feminine `-a` → `masculine/a`

  * `alto/a`, `bajo/a`, `bonito/a`
* Masculine consonant + feminine `-a` → `masculine(a)`

  * `trabajador(a)`, `español(a)`
* Distinct forms → `masculine/feminine` with NO spaces.
* Invariable adjectives remain unchanged.

  * `grande`, `feliz`, `amable`, `interesante`, `inteligente`
* Never determine formatting from spelling alone.
* `gender` and `gender_type` MUST always be `null` for adjectives.
